Step 1 · Learn

Know Your Skin Type

Your skin type is decided mostly by genetics and how much oil (sebum) your skin makes. Not sure what yours is? Here's the beginner-friendly breakdown.

✨

Normal

Balanced and happy. Not too oily, not too dry. Small pores, few breakouts, and a smooth, even texture. Lucky you!

💧

Dry

Feels tight, rough, or flaky — especially after cleansing. May look dull or show fine lines. Loves rich creams and gentle cleansers.

🌟

Oily

Shiny all over, bigger pores, and makeup that slides off by noon. Prone to blackheads and breakouts, but ages gracefully!

🌗

Combination

The most common type! Oily or shiny in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) but normal or dry on the cheeks. Needs balanced care.

🌸

Sensitive

Easily flushed, itchy, stings, or turns red with new products or weather changes. Needs fragrance-free, calming formulas.

🔍 The 60-Second Skin Test

No tools needed — just your bare face and a little patience. Do this at night before bed.

1

Cleanse gently. Wash your face with a mild cleanser and pat dry with a soft towel.

2

Wait 30–60 minutes. Don't apply anything. Just let your skin be itself.

3

Observe how it feels & looks. Tight or flaky? You lean dry. Shiny everywhere? Oily. Shiny only on forehead/nose/chin? Combination. Comfortable and even? Normal. Red or reactive? Sensitive.

Step 2 · Understand

Common Skin Concerns

You can have a skin type AND concerns — they're different things. These are the most common issues beginners face, and the good news: all of them can be improved.

🫘

Acne & Breakouts

Caused by clogged pores, excess oil, and bacteria. Treated with salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide, and consistency.

⚫

Blackheads

Oxidized oil stuck in pores. Gentle exfoliation with BHA (salicylic acid) and never squeezing them helps most.

🟤

Dark Spots

Post-acne marks or pigmentation left behind. Brighteners like niacinamide and vitamin C fade them over time.

🏜️

Dryness

A damaged moisture barrier. Look for ceramides, hyaluronic acid, and glycerin — and skip harsh foaming cleansers.

🌫️

Dullness

Dead skin cells and dehydration stealing your glow. Light exfoliation and hydration bring the radiance back.
